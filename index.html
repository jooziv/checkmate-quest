How to play

You play White. Tap one of your pieces to see where it can go, then tap a square to move.

Mate in 1: find the one move that checkmates. Mate in 2 or 3: make a move, the computer answers with Black's best defense, and you keep going until checkmate. Each move has to keep the win on track.

Every puzzle has exactly one winning first move. Solve with no mistakes and no hints for ⭐⭐⭐.
